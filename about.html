About

Academic, researcher and teacher

I am a political scientist studying how institutions, security actors and societies shape the prospects for peace after violence.

My academic journey

I am based at Corvinus University of Budapest. Previously I worked at the University of Kent, where I served as Head of School, and at the German Institute for Global and Area Studies.

I earned my PhD in Political Science at Freie Universität Berlin. My research combines empirical work in conflict-affected settings with broader debates about institutions, peacebuilding and international order.

Research and teaching

My academic work spans peace and conflict studies, security sector reform, institutional change, international norms and research-led teaching.

Beyond academia

Outside academia, I enjoy exploring new places by bicycle, spending time outdoors and discovering good food—preferably vegan.
