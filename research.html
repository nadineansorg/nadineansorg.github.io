Research

Research themes

My research examines institutions, security actors and societies in contexts of conflict and political change, with a focus on peacebuilding, legitimacy, trust and international norms.

01

Peacebuilding and post-conflict institutions

Institutional reform, security sector reform, state–society relations and the conditions for sustainable peace.

02

Security, trust and legitimacy

Police reform, public trust, accountability and relationships between security providers and communities.

03

Forgiveness, reconciliation and social relations

How individuals and communities navigate legacies of violence, including forgiveness without closure.

04

International norms and institutional change

Norm contestation, deinstitutionalisation and the changing role of international institutions.
