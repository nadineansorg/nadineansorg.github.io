Teaching & supervision

Teaching and supervision

I teach peace and conflict studies, security and international relations, with an emphasis on connecting theory to real political challenges.

In my teaching, I encourage students to question established explanations, assess evidence critically and develop their own arguments. I value discussion, curiosity and the opportunity to explore complex problems from different perspectives.

Areas of teaching

01

Conflict analysis and peacebuilding

Why do conflicts begin, persist and end? How can societies build a more sustainable peace after violence?

02

Security studies

Traditional and non-traditional approaches to security, including human security, security governance and security sector reform.

03

Mediation and negotiation

How parties negotiate under pressure, manage competing interests and seek ways out of conflict.

04

Research methods

Designing research questions, comparing cases and evaluating evidence in qualitative and comparative research.

Teaching experience

At the University of Kent, my teaching included negotiation, peacebuilding, mediation, and war, violence and peace.

At Corvinus University of Budapest, I am preparing a Security Studies course that brings together established debates with questions of human security, institutions and peacebuilding.

Doctoral supervision

I supervise doctoral research in peace and conflict studies, security governance, institutional reform and related areas of international relations and comparative politics.

I am particularly interested in projects that combine strong conceptual questions with careful empirical research.

I served as Head of the School of Politics and International Relations at the University of Kent from 2021 to 2023. My academic work also includes postgraduate education, academic governance and supporting research and teaching communities.
